<strong>Atlanta United 2</strong> secured a 2-1 victory against <strong>Carolina Core FC</strong> in their MLS NEXT Pro regular season home opener on March 8, 2026. The home side took the lead in the 32nd minute through a goal from <strong>Cayman Togashi</strong>. <strong>Carolina Core FC</strong> equalized shortly after the second-half whistle, in the 46th minute, due to an own goal by Atlanta’s Matthew Senanou. However, <strong>Daniel Chica</strong> ultimately scored the decisive goal for Atlanta United 2 in the 62nd minute, restoring their lead and ensuring a successful start to their 2026 season at home.

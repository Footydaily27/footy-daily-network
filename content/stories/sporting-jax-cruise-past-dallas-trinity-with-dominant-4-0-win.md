Sporting JAX made a statement on the day with an easy 4-0 win over Dallas Trinity in a one-sided encounter.

The breakthrough for JAX came moments before halftime with Baylee DeSmit (45’) opening the scoring and putting JAX ahead in a match where every goal mattered. Just minutes later, in stoppage time, Paige Kenton (45+2’) doubled JAX’s lead to ensure that JAX went into halftime with all three points within their grasp.

In the second half, JAX continued to dominate and dictate the pace of the game. JAX’s pressure up front eventually paid off with Sophia Boman adding to the lead.

Meg Hughes scored the fourth goal to round off the performance for Sporting JAX.

Dallas Trinity struggled to find any sort of rhythm throughout the game, with Sporting JAX keeping their defense tight and their offense sharp.

It was a complete performance by Sporting JAX, who can be proud of themselves as they head home with a convincing 4-0 win.

Chattanooga FC emerged victorious with a 2-0 win over Columbus Crew 2 in a game of patience, persistence, and an outstanding performance by Ashton Gordon.

The game took an unexpected turn in the 2nd minute of the game when Columbus Crew 2’s goalkeeper was sent off, leaving them with 10 men for almost the entire 90 minutes. Chattanooga FC took charge of the game, possessing the ball and creating many scoring opportunities, although failing to break the deadlock in the opening minutes.

Despite being down a man, Columbus Crew 2 showed character and almost took the lead before halftime with a free kick that struck the crossbar.

However, Chattanooga’s pressure finally paid off in the latter stages of the match:<br>
• 87’ – K. Ancelin scored the opening goal (Assisted by Ashton Gordon)<br>
• 90+5’ – Alex McGrath secured the win (Assisted by Ashton Gordon)

It was evident that Ashton Gordon was the key factor in this match, contributing to both goals and playing an instrumental role in breaking down Columbus’s defense.

In the end, Chattanooga FC’s dominance and pressure in the latter stages of the match saw them win 2-0, with Columbus Crew 2’s initial red card being too much to overcome.

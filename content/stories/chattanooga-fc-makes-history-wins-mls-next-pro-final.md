Chattanooga FC are MLS NEXT Pro champions after beating LAFC 2, 1–0, Sunday night at Finley Stadium. A record crowd of 10,228 watched CFC become the first independent club to win the title. 

Damien Barker John scored the winner in the 31st minute with a strike from outside the box. He was named the final’s MVP after delivering the biggest goal of Chattanooga’s season. 

LAFC 2 pushed hard in the second half, but Chattanooga goalkeeper Eldin Jakupović made six saves to protect the lead. A late bicycle kick from Marius Aiyenero was ruled out for offside, and CFC held on through the final whistle. 

Three seasons after joining MLS NEXT Pro, Chattanooga lifted the trophy in front of its home fans—a historic finish to a season they won’t forget.

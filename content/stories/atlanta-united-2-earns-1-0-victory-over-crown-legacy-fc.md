Atlanta United 2 claimed a hard-fought 1-0 win over Crown Legacy FC on Saturday, September 20, 2025. The match was tightly contested from the start, with both sides creating chances but struggling to find a breakthrough.

The decisive moment came in the second half when Atlanta capitalized on a well-worked attacking move to grab the only goal of the match. From there, the defense stood strong, keeping Crown Legacy at bay and preserving the clean sheet.

With the win, Atlanta United 2 picked up an important three points as the regular season winds down, continuing to build momentum in MLS NEXT Pro competition.

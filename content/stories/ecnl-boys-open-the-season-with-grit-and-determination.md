The ECNL Boys officially kicked off their first game of the season, and they wasted no time setting the tone for the year ahead. From the first whistle, the boys showed discipline, intensity, and an unwavering drive to secure the all-important three points. Every challenge was met head-on, every 50/50 ball was contested, and the team’s collective effort never wavered until the final whistle.

What stood out most was not just the physical battle, but the hunger and commitment the group displayed. The boys worked for each other across all areas of the pitch pressing high, staying organized defensively, and creating dangerous chances going forward. Even in the most demanding moments, their determination and resilience proved that this is a team ready to compete with anyone this season.

This opening match was more than just a game it was a statement of intent. If the fight and passion on display are any indication, the ECNL Boys are prepared for an exciting, competitive, and inspiring season ahead.

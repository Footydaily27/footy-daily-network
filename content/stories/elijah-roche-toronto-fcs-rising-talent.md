Toronto FC’s rising talent Elijah Roche, 17, continues to make his mark as one of Canada’s brightest young defenders. The Canadian youth international and MLS Next All-Star alumnus impressed with his composure, positioning, and technical ability on the ball — qualities that have made him one of the top prospects in the TFC academy system.<br>


Roche made his MLS Next Pro debut on September 12, 2025, for Toronto FC II in a hard-fought 2–1 loss to New England Revolution II, showcasing maturity beyond his years in his first professional appearance. As one of the standout prospects in the Toronto FC pipeline, Elijah Roche’s journey is only beginning — and all signs point toward a bright future ahead. 💫

Kalonji Pro-Profile earned a 1–0 victory over Georgia Revolution FC on December 14 in the Georgia Final of the United Premier Soccer League (UPSL) National Finals, securing advancement in the postseason competition.

The closely contested match featured strong defensive organization from both sides, with limited scoring opportunities in the early stages. Kalonji Pro-Profile found the breakthrough late in the first half, capitalizing on sustained pressure to score the decisive goal.

Georgia Revolution FC responded with increased attacking intent after the interval, pushing numbers forward in search of an equalizer. Despite several promising sequences in the final third, Kalonji’s back line remained composed, while disciplined game management and solid goalkeeping preserved the narrow advantage.

As the match progressed, intensity rose on both sides, reflecting the high stakes of the UPSL postseason. Kalonji Pro-Profile successfully closed out the contest to claim the Georgia Final and continue its campaign in the National Finals.

The result underscores Kalonji Pro-Profile’s consistent form and highlights the competitive standard of UPSL playoff soccer in the region.

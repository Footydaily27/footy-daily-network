Kansas City, MO — Sporting Kansas City staged an impressive comeback Friday night, battling back from a two–goal deficit to secure a 2–2 draw against Columbus Crew in an exciting Major League Soccer clash at Children’s Mercy Park.

Columbus came out strong in the opening half, scoring twice to take early control of the match. Their quick passing and aggressive attack put the Sporting defense under pressure and gave the visitors a comfortable lead heading toward halftime.

However, Sporting Kansas City responded with determination in the second half. Forward Dejan Joveljić sparked the comeback, scoring two crucial goals that brought the home crowd back to life and leveled the match at 2–2.

The game’s most dramatic moment came late in the second half when Sporting goalkeeper John Pulskamp made a crucial penalty save in the 87th minute, preventing Columbus from reclaiming the lead and securing the draw for his team.

Despite missed opportunities on both sides in the closing minutes, the match ended in a stalemate. The result leaves both teams with valuable points as they continue their early-season campaigns in Major League Soccer.

Fans at Children’s Mercy Park were treated to a thrilling contest that showcased resilience from Sporting Kansas City and attacking strength from Columbus Crew, setting the stage for an exciting MLS season ahead.

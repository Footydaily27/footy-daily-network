In an impressive showing, Crown Legacy FC registered a 2-0 victory against Atlanta United 2, making use of their chances and setting the pace for the entire game.

The first goal was scored by Crown Legacy’s forward Hugo Mbongue when he converted the ball into the back of the net after an elaborate move. The build-up began from Nathan Richmond, who provided an assist to Mbongue to make it 1-0 for Crown Legacy.

The lead of Crown Legacy FC was doubled by Henry Kessler as they scored late into the game. This goal was scored in the attacking third by the defender from Charlotte FC. Once again, Nathan Richmond provided the final assist for this goal too.

Atlanta United 2 FC tried hard to get back into the game but found it difficult to break the tight formation of Crown Legacy. Meanwhile, Crown Legacy FC continued to be dangerous whenever they went forward. Ultimately, Crown Legacy’s ability to score efficiently coupled with the excellent performance of Richmond gave them the win.

Final Score: Crown Legacy FC 2-0 Atlanta United 2

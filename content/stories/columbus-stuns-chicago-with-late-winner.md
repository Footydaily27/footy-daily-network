**Columbus Stuns Chicago With Late Winner**  
*By David Levinsohn | Footy Daily Network*

Chicago led three times, but Columbus kept coming back.

Brais Méndez scored in the 93rd minute to give the Columbus Crew a dramatic 4–3 win over the Chicago Fire at SeatGeek Stadium on November 7. 

Robert Lewandowski converted two first-half penalties, with Gonzalo Tapia and Anass Zaroury answering for Columbus. Maren Haile-Selassie then finished a rebound in stoppage time to send Chicago into halftime ahead 3–2. 

Tapia struck again in the 62nd minute before Méndez delivered the late winner, securing seventh place in the Eastern Conference for Columbus.

Despite the loss, Chicago finished fourth with 55 points and will face Charlotte FC in the opening round of the playoffs. The Fire showed their attacking quality, but letting three leads slip leaves plenty to address before the postseason.

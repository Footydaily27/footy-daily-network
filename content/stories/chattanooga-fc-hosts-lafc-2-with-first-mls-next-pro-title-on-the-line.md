Chattanooga FC nearly saw its season end last weekend. Now, the club is preparing to host the biggest match of its MLS NEXT Pro run.

Chattanooga faces LAFC 2 at Finley Stadium on Sunday, November 8, at 6 p.m. ET. Both teams are making their first appearance in the final, with Chattanooga becoming the first independent club to reach this stage. (++[mlsnextpro.com](http://mlsnextpro.com)++⁠)

For the home supporters, it will be hard to top the emotions of the Eastern Conference Final. Chattanooga fell behind Columbus Crew 2 by two goals before Alex McGrath scored just before halftime. Damien Barker John tied the match in the 71st minute, giving the hosts time to find a winner.

That moment came deep into stoppage time. Farid Sar-Sar met Tate Robertson’s cross with a header to complete the 3–2 comeback and send Finley Stadium into celebration. (++[fe-en.mnp-prd.deltatre.digital](http://fe-en.mnp-prd.deltatre.digital)++⁠)

Chattanooga will have the crowd behind it again Sunday, but LAFC 2 has already shown it can handle playing away from home.

The Western Conference’s No. 6 seed has won three straight road playoff matches against higher-seeded opponents. After advancing past Houston Dynamo 2, LAFC 2 beat Austin FC II and Ventura County FC by matching 1–0 scores. Marius Aiyenero scored the winner against Ventura County to book the club’s spot in the final. (++[mlsnextpro.com](http://mlsnextpro.com)++⁠)

Chattanooga will want to give its supporters something to cheer about early. Another slow start would leave the hosts chasing a team that has protected narrow leads in its last two matches.

For LAFC 2, there is little reason to be intimidated by another away trip. The visitors have spent the entire postseason finding ways to win in that situation.

Chattanooga has the chance to finish the job at home. LAFC 2 has the chance to pull off one more upset. Either way, a new champion will lift the trophy Sunday night.

The match will stream live on ++[MLSNEXTPro.com](http://MLSNEXTPro.com)++ and OneFootball. (++[mlsnextpro.com](http://mlsnextpro.com)++⁠)

Chattanooga FC have made history.

The Boys in Blue secured their place in the MLS NEXT Pro Cup Final after delivering a dramatic 3-2 comeback victory over Columbus Crew 2 in the Eastern Conference Final.



Trailing 2-0, Chattanooga refused to back down, fighting their way back into the match before completing the comeback with a dramatic stoppage-time winner from Farid Sar-Sar.

The victory marks a historic achievement for the club, as Chattanooga FC become the first independent club in MLS NEXT Pro history to reach the league’s championship final.

For a club that has continued to challenge expectations, Sunday’s result represents another major milestone in its journey.

Now, just one match stands between Chattanooga FC and MLS NEXT Pro glory.

**One club. One final. One chance to make even more history.**

In a shocking start to the new UPSL Georgia season, newly promoted Atlético Buford Futbol Club announced their arrival in emphatic fashion with a 8–3 victory over powerhouse Kalonji Pro-Profile.

The debutants wasted no time making a statement, overwhelming Kalonji with relentless attacking play and clinical finishing. Despite Kalonji’s reputation as one of the conference’s most consistent sides, they had no answer to Buford’s pace and intensity.

The win marks Atlético Buford’s first match at the UPSL Premier Division level, and they couldn’t have scripted a more memorable introduction. For Kalonji, the heavy defeat raises questions about their early-season form and sets the stage for a crucial response in the matches to come.

Atlético Buford will look to build on this historic triumph, while Kalonji Pro-Profile face the challenge of bouncing back quickly in what promises to be a fiercely competitive season.

2026 US Open Cup Qualifier | October 12, 2025

In a thrilling U.S. Open Cup qualifying clash, Kalonji Pro-Profile emerged victorious with a 4–2 win over LSA Norcross (Limeno Georgia) on October 12, 2025. The matchup, held in Snellville, Georgia, showcased end-to-end action, tactical grit, and a dramatic finale that had fans on their feet.

Both teams came out firing, trading early chances and goals in a fast-paced first half. LSA Norcross showed great composure in attack, but Kalonji’s experience and attacking chemistry kept them in control. The scoreline remained tight deep into the second half, with momentum shifting between both sides.

As the match edged into stoppage time, Zain Hylton delivered the decisive moment — firing home in the 90+3′ minute to secure Kalonji’s place in the next round of the 2026 U.S. Open Cup Qualifying Tournament. The 4–2 final score reflected both the attacking quality and fighting spirit of the home side.

With this result, Kalonji Pro-Profile advances to the next stage of qualifying, while LSA Norcross exits after a strong showing in their debut campaign under the new club identity.

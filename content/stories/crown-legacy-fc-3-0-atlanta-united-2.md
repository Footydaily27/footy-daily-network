Atlanta United 2 kicked off the game strongly, maintaining possession of the ball and creating some good scoring opportunities in the first half. Even though they were able to make some saves, they were unable to capitalize on any of the scoring chances. This meant that the game was still 0-0 at halftime. On the defensive front, Atlanta United 2 was also solid, keeping Crown Legacy FC at bay as none of Crown’s shots were on target in the first half.<br>
The game was to take a major turn in the second half when Adrian Gill was given a second yellow card in the 55th minute, resulting in Atlanta United 2 playing the rest of the game with 10 players.

Crown Legacy FC capitalized on the one-man advantage soon after. In the 61st minute, Nimfasha Berchimas opened the scoring, reacting well to score from a rebound, making it 1-0.

As the game progressed, Crown Legacy continued to capitalize on their lead. In the 84th minute, Rodolfo Aloko scored his second goal of the game, doubling Crown Legacy’s lead. Finally, in stoppage time, Magic Smalls finished off the game with a strong effort to seal off a 3-0 victory.

Atlanta United 2 will be disappointed at not being able to capitalize on a strong first half. However, the red card was a turning point in the game. Crown Legacy demonstrated patience and quality in the second half, scoring from Berchimas, Aloko, and Smalls to win the game.

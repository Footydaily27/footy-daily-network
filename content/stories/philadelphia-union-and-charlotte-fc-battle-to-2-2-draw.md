Philadelphia Union and Charlotte FC battled to a 2-2 draw on October 17 at Subaru Park, with both teams looking to build momentum heading into the MLS Cup Playoffs.

Philadelphia got things started in the 24th minute when Milan Iloski scored from the penalty spot to give the Union a 1-0 lead.

Charlotte came out strong after halftime. Rodolfo Aloko tied the game in the 48th minute before Idan Toklomati scored in the 62nd minute to put Charlotte ahead 2-1.

But Philadelphia wasn't finished. Bruno Damiani found the back of the net in the 73rd minute to tie things up. Both teams pushed for a late winner, but the match ended 2-2.

The draw ended Philadelphia's eight-game winning streak but extended its unbeaten run to 15 matches.

With the MLS Cup Playoffs right around the corner, both teams have plenty to play for. Charlotte finished the regular season fifth in the Eastern Conference, while Philadelphia secured the sixth seed.

Charlotte will face Chicago Fire in the first round, while Philadelphia will take on New England Revolution. Both teams will look to carry their strong form into the postseason as they chase an MLS Cup title.

The playoffs begin November 19, with MLS Cup set for December 18.

New England Revolution II managed to come out on top in a closely contested match against CT United FC. The game started off well for both teams, with each side pressing forward and looking to score early goals. New England Revolution II eventually found their footing and started to play some good football, keeping possession and moving forward through midfield.

The turning point came when Revolution II capitalized on their movement, converting a well-worked opportunity to take the lead. The movement in the final third and passing movement were too much for CT United FC to handle.

CT United FC launched a reaction to this, pushing numbers forward to look for an equalizing goal. However, New England’s backline kept them at bay, maintaining shape even when pressed.

As the game went on, the pace remained intense. Revolution II kept a tight game in the final minutes, denying CT United FC opportunities while continuing to threaten on the break.

In the end, New England Revolution II won the game, keeping their cool and composure against a determined CT United FC side.

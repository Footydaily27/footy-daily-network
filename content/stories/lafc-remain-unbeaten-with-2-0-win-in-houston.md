Los Angeles FC earned a convincing 2–0 road victory over Houston Dynamo FC on Saturday night, maintaining their perfect start in Major League Soccer.

After a tightly contested opening half, LAFC broke through in the second half before adding a late goal to put the match away. The defense held firm to secure the clean sheet.

With momentum building, LAFC are quickly establishing themselves as one of the league’s early contenders.

Atlanta United 2 staged a great comeback to beat Huntsville City FC 4-1 in a highly competitive and exciting encounter in MLS NEXT Pro.

Huntsville City FC started by scoring through Leo Christiano, but Atlanta United 2 showed great character by equalizing through Liam Butts. The equalizer gave Atlanta United 2 momentum as they looked to take control of the game.

Luke Brennan scored to put Atlanta United 2 in the lead, but Liam Butts scored his second goal of the night to extend their lead. The night was rounded out with Enzo Dovlo’s first professional goal as Atlanta United 2 sealed a great victory.

The game was a true reflection of the attacking prowess both teams possess, but Atlanta United 2 showed great finishing and control to take all three points at home.
